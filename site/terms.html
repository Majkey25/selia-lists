Files and limitations

Keep backups of important work. Deletion and replacement during backup restoration can remove local content permanently. Check exports before relying on them. PDF export creates a separate, rasterized copy with annotations; it does not edit the original PDF's text or vector objects. SeliaLists has no cloud synchronization.
